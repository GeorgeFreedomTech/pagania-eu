## History beyond the pages

Pagania explores ancient cultures, traditional knowledge and practical skills, with a particular focus on the **Early Middle Ages** and **pre-Christian European traditions** - especially the **Vikings, Celts and Slavs**.

Through lectures, workshops, historical games and live demonstrations, Pagania brings the cultural context and practical knowledge of the past closer to modern audiences.

Originally established in 2017 as a historical goods and educational project, Pagania now focuses primarily on consultations, workshops and lectures for cultural institutions, educational projects, event organisers and individuals.

*Pagania is an independent one-person project. You work directly with its founder - from the first conversation to the final delivery.*
