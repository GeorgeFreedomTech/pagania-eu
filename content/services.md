## I. Consultations

**Expert insight for your project.**

Expert consultations and subject-specific support for film and television productions, museums, cultural institutions, educational projects and event organisers.

Topics include cultural aspects, everyday life, old traditions, games, symbolism, practical skills and historical equipment.

## II. Workshops

**Live the Past.**

Hands-on workshops connecting historical knowledge with active participation.

Topics include historical board games, divination systems, fire-making, traditional skills, self-defence, physical training and survival. Historical clothing and equipment can be incorporated.

## III. Lectures & Demonstrations

**Historical cultures, traditions and skills.**

Lectures and live demonstrations covering historical games, Vikings, Celts, Slavs, pre-Christian traditions, divination, everyday life and survival.

Presentations can combine expert insight with demonstrations of historical objects, clothing, tools, weapons and practical techniques.

## IV. Tailored Programmes

Lectures, demonstrations and workshops can be adapted to the subject, audience, setting and goals of each project - from a focused expert presentation to a maximum hands-on experience.
